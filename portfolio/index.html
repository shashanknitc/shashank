# About

I am an organic chemist with expertise spanning synthetic organic chemistry, glycochemistry, biocatalysis, nanotechnology and chemical biology. My research focuses on designing and synthesizing functional molecules and catalytic systems that address challenges at the interface of chemistry and biology.

I earned my Ph.D. in Organic Chemistry from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi under the supervision of Prof. Ravi P. Singh. My doctoral research centred on the synthesis of heterocycles and oligosaccharides, providing a strong foundation in synthetic methodology, molecular design, reaction mechanisms and advanced structural characterisation using modern analytical techniques.

Following my doctoral studies, I joined Iowa State University as a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the laboratory of Prof. Yan Zhao. There, I expanded my research into glycosynthase engineering and glycoacylation, developing libraries of biocatalyst–nanoparticle conjugates and demonstrating their catalytic applications. This interdisciplinary work combined organic synthesis, enzymology and nanotechnology to create innovative catalytic platforms.

I further broadened my expertise in chemical biology during my postdoctoral research in the laboratory of Prof. Maja Köhn at the University of Bonn, where I explored the application of chemical tools to understand and manipulate biological processes.

My research interests encompass synthetic organic chemistry, glycochemistry, chemical biology, biocatalysis and nanomaterials. I am passionate about developing innovative molecular systems that bridge fundamental chemistry with real-world applications in catalysis, biotechnology and medicine.

# Research interests

Designing and synthesising functional molecules and catalytic systems that address challenges at the interface of chemistry and biology.

Core

Synthetic organic chemistry

Methodology, molecular design and reaction mechanisms — the foundation the rest of the group is built on. Heterocycle synthesis and strategic bond construction for biologically relevant targets.

Core

Glycochemistry

Synthesis and study of oligosaccharides and glycoconjugates, with an emphasis on glycosynthase engineering and glycoacylation for biological interrogation.

Active

Biocatalysis

Engineered enzymes as selective, mild catalysts. Developing libraries of biocatalyst–nanoparticle conjugates and demonstrating their catalytic applications.

Active

Nanotechnology

Nanomaterials as catalytic platforms — immobilisation, hybrid catalysts and the intersection of inorganic materials with organic synthesis.

Active

Chemical biology

Applying chemical tools to understand and manipulate biological processes — bridging fundamental chemistry with real-world applications in medicine and biotechnology.

Methods

Characterisation

Advanced analytical and structural techniques for molecular characterisation and mechanistic study, developed throughout doctoral and postdoctoral work.
